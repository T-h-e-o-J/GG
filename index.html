Goonius Gardeners is a garden maintenance group run by three young students. We’re building practical skills, taking pride in our work, and helping Dunedin homes feel a bit more looked after.

Small job or full garden reset, we bring the same attitude: turn up, work hard, and leave the place better than we found it.

Privacy notice

We collect your name, contact details, and garden enquiry so Goonius Gardeners can respond to your request and prepare a quote. Please do not include sensitive information or someone else’s details without their permission.

Form submissions are sent through FormSubmit to gooniusgardeners@gmail.com. We use the information only for your enquiry and keep it only as long as reasonably needed. You can ask to access or correct your information by contacting us at gooniusgardeners@gmail.com.

For children and young people, a parent or guardian should submit the enquiry or give permission before contact details are provided.
