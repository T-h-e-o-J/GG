Why Goonius?

We’re growing
something good.

Goonius Gardeners is a garden maintenance group run by three young students. We’re putting in the hours to raise money, learn useful skills, and help Dunedin homes feel a bit more looked after.

Small job or full garden reset, we bring the same attitude: turn up, work hard, and leave the place better than we found it.
